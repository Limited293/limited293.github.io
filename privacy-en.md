UtaShirabe (the “App”) is a karaoke song management app. The App handles information about users only to the extent necessary to provide its services.

## 1. Information We Collect

The App generates and uses an anonymous user identifier to provide its services. The following information may also be stored as you use the App:

- Songs added to My Songs
- Key and octave settings for each song
- Singing history
- Optional karaoke scores and karaoke machine information
- Dates and times of use
- Feedback submitted by users
- An optional reply email address, only when the user requests a response

At present, the App does not require users to provide their name, email address, phone number, postal address, contacts, location, photos, videos, or audio recordings for normal use.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

- Providing App features
- Saving and displaying each user’s My Songs, singing history, and settings
- Maintaining the safety and stability of the App and addressing issues
- Reviewing user feedback and improving the App
- Responding by email when a user requests a reply

A reply email address is not used for advertising, sales, or marketing; it is used only to respond to or clarify the related feedback.

## 3. External Services

The App uses Supabase for authentication, data storage, and related functions. It may also use Spotify and other external services to search for and match song information.

When using external services, information is handled only to the extent necessary to provide the App.

## 4. Advertising Tracking

At present, the App does not use collected user information to track users for third-party advertising.

## 5. Data Storage and Deletion

Users can start account deletion using the account deletion feature provided in the App.

When an account is deleted, the following user data associated with that account is deleted from the App’s database, except where retention is required by law or similar obligations:

- My Songs
- Singing history
- Feedback
- Reply email addresses stored with feedback
- Other settings and account information linked only to that user

If a user requests a reply and support is actually provided by email, sent and received email may be stored separately from the App’s database by the email service used, as a record of support correspondence. This information is handled only as necessary for responding to inquiries, security checks, legal obligations, and similar purposes; it is not intended to be retained permanently when no longer needed.

The following shared song data is not personal account data belonging to a specific user and is therefore not included in account deletion:

- Song and artist names
- External song identifiers
- Song pitch-range information
- Other shared catalog information

## 6. Sharing with Third Parties

Except where required by law, we do not sell user data to third parties beyond the use of service providers and external services necessary to provide the App.

## 7. Security

The App restricts access to each user’s data and takes measures to prevent unauthorized access and data leaks.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be revised in response to new features or changes in laws or external services. Important changes will be announced in the App or by another appropriate method.
