Data handling differs by app. Review the policy for the app you use.

UtaShirabe handles information such as anonymous user identifiers, My Songs, singing history, feedback, and tip-purchase information, and may use Supabase, Spotify and other external services, Apple App Store, and StoreKit.

Arrivibe uses location for approach detection and Apple MapKit for destination search. It stores destinations, monitoring state, and related information on the device. Information may be processed by Apple services.
